50,000 prompts/day
  configuration                               $/day       $/year     saved/yr
  naive: interactive, dynamic thinking        57.82       21,103            0
  thinking off                                14.42        5,262       15,841
  thinking off + caching                      14.08        5,139       15,964
  thinking off + batch                         7.21        2,631       18,472
  thinking off + batch + caching               7.06        2,576       18,527

Notes
  - Token counts are measured from live runs, not estimated.
  - Batch trades up to 24h turnaround for ~50% off. A daily sweep can absorb that.
  - Cache and batch discounts do not stack; the larger applies to cached tokens.
  - Output dominates: it is ~7x the input rate and, with thinking on, ~14x the volume.
  - Throughput is not a constraint at these volumes. A 50,000-prompt day
    completes in under 4 minutes at the measured service capacity.
